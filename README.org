#+TITLE: dseams-plumed
#+OPTIONS: toc:nil

The d-SEAMS topological ice score as a PLUMED action.

=DSEAMS_CAGES= takes a group of oxygen atoms and, every step, builds the
mutual and union four-nearest-neighbour graphs, the primitive six-membered
rings and the seeded cage assignment of d-SEAMS 2.0. With =COMPLETE= it
fills the last vertex of six-rings whose other vertices carry a label.
The action reports cage occupancy, the largest connected cluster, the
split into ice Ic, ice Ih and mixed, and the four-neighbour star method
=CHILL+= on the same frame. Integer counts without derivatives drive
=PRINT=, =COMMITTOR= basins and stopping conditions. Cage lengths
inside the engine use the unit A. =LENGTH_SCALE= converts from the
PLUMED length unit (10 for the default nm, 1 after =UNITS LENGTH=A=).

#+begin_src plumed
LOAD FILE=libdseams_plumed.so
ice: DSEAMS_CAGES ATOMS=1-4096 CUTOFF=3.5 COMPLETE
PRINT ARG=ice.nice,ice.nmax,ice.chillice,ice.chillmax STRIDE=100 FILE=ICE
COMMITTOR ARG=ice.nmax STRIDE=100 BASIN_LL1=0 BASIN_UL1=20 BASIN_LL2=800 BASIN_UL2=100000
#+end_src

* Install

Build the shared module against an installed PLUMED. Meson runs
=plumed info --include-dir= for the headers. The module links
=libplumedKernel=, which is what a GROMACS build needs when it dlopens
PLUMED locally. An installed =seams-core= (=pkg-config seams-core=)
supplies the engine; the wrap is the fallback.

With pixi (plumed, compilers, meson):

#+begin_src bash
pixi install
pixi run build          # build/libdseams_plumed.so
pixi run test
#+end_src

Without pixi, put an installed =plumed= on =PATH= and run:

#+begin_src bash
meson setup build --buildtype=release
meson compile -C build
meson install -C build   # libdseams_plumed.so into libdir
#+end_src

=LOAD FILE== takes that path, or a relative path to
=build/libdseams_plumed.so= from the working directory of the run.

* Keywords

=ATOMS= (required) names the oxygen atoms of the water molecules.

=CUTOFF= (default 3.5) sets the neighbour cutoff in A for the =CHILL+= graph.

=CANDIDATE= (default 5.5) sets the candidate shell in A for the four-nearest walk.

=K= (default 4) sets the neighbours per molecule in the bonded graph.

=LENGTH_SCALE= (default 10.0) converts the PLUMED length unit to A.

=COMPLETE= (flag, off) fills the last vertex of six-rings whose other
vertices carry a label.

=IONS= names ions read against the water assignment. Those ions stay
off the graph.

=ION_CUTOFF= (default 3.5) sets the radius in A of an ion's first water shell.

=HOPS= (default 3) sets the bonds from the centre in each local topology key.

=GUESTS= lists one atom per guest molecule (methane carbon, THF oxygen,
an ion) and needs =SIGNATURE=: each guest goes to the nearest cage
centre (the periodic centroid of a cage's vertices) within
=GUEST_RADIUS= (default 4 A). =noccupied= counts cages with a guest,
=nmultiple= cages with more than one, =nfreeguest= guests in no cage:
the filled-cage count that hydrate nucleation studies bias and commit
on.

=LIBRARY= names a topology key library from =seams fingerprint --emit-library=,
or several, comma separated, built at different hop counts: each molecule
takes the label of the deepest library that holds its key, so a molecule
whose wide neighbourhood is disturbed still gets a name from its inner
shells.

Omit =LIBRARY= or =IONS= when unused. The deepest library must be built
at =HOPS=; two libraries at one hop count, or libraries that differ in
colouring, are errors.

* Ice fraction

=DSEAMS_CHILL= is the CHILL+ ice fraction on its own, without the cage
walk. =RULE=chill+= (the default) is the published Nguyen and Molinero
2015 rule: q_3 averaged over the four nearest neighbours inside the
cutoff, closed edges (=c_ij <= -0.8= staggered, =-0.35 <= c_ij <= 0.25=
eclipsed), and the interfacial neighbour test. =RULE=ovito-chill+= is
the OVITO 3.17.2 Chill+ modifier: q_3 over the whole cutoff shell, open
edges, and no neighbour test. =fraction= is (cubic + hexagonal) / N.
=ncubic=, =nhex=, =ninterfacial=, =nclathrate=, =ninterclath=, =nwater=
and =nmax= are the class counts and the largest bulk-ice cluster.

Those labels are a step function. No derivative exists, and the action
does not return one. =METAD= and the restraints refuse them. Use them
for =PRINT= and =COMMITTOR=.

#+begin_src plumed
UNITS LENGTH=A
ice: DSEAMS_CHILL ATOMS=1-4096 CUTOFF=3.5 LENGTH_SCALE=1.0
PRINT ARG=ice.fraction,ice.nice,ice.nmax STRIDE=100 FILE=ICE
ovito: DSEAMS_CHILL ATOMS=1-4096 RULE=ovito-chill+ LENGTH_SCALE=1.0
PRINT ARG=ovito.fraction,ovito.ninterfacial STRIDE=100 FILE=OVITO
#+end_src

* Smooth fraction

=DSEAMS_SMOOTH_CHILL= is the collective variable to bias. It keeps the
q_3 correlation of CHILL+ and replaces the hard cutoff and the hard
thresholds by smooth steps at the published values. A bond is staggered
below -0.8 and eclipsed between -0.35 and 0.25. A molecule scores by a
soft OR of the published counts: four staggered neighbours (cubic), or
three staggered and one eclipsed (hexagonal). The value is the mean
molecule score. Derivatives are analytic. On a ladder of ice Ic slabs
the smooth fraction tracks the published one with an rms residual of
0.010, and it does not decrease as the published fraction increases.
Ideal ice Ih scores 0.984 where the published fraction is 1. The fit
is =docs/smooth-fit.png=.

#+begin_src plumed
UNITS LENGTH=A
ice: DSEAMS_SMOOTH_CHILL ATOMS=1-4096 D_0=3.2 D_MAX=3.5 LENGTH_SCALE=1.0
metad: METAD ARG=ice SIGMA=0.05 HEIGHT=0.2 PACE=500 GRID_MIN=0 GRID_MAX=1 GRID_BIN=200 FILE=HILLS
PRINT ARG=ice,metad.bias STRIDE=100 FILE=COLVAR
#+end_src

* Ring census

=DSEAMS_RINGS= counts primitive rings on the cutoff neighbour graph
(Franzblau shortest-path rings, =MAX= from 3 to 8, default 6). =n3=
through =n8= are those counts. This is not =DSEAMS_CAGES= =sixrings=,
which is counted on the union of the four-nearest graphs. The two
graphs coincide on an undistorted tetrahedral lattice. The counts have
no derivatives.

#+begin_src plumed
UNITS LENGTH=A
rings: DSEAMS_RINGS ATOMS=1-4096 CUTOFF=3.5 MAX=6 LENGTH_SCALE=1.0
PRINT ARG=rings.n5,rings.n6 STRIDE=100 FILE=RINGS
#+end_src

* Components

Each component reports an integer count with no derivative.

=nice= is the occupancy of hexagonal or double-diamond cages.
=nmax= is the largest connected cluster of cage molecules.
=nclus= is the number of connected cage clusters.
=nic= counts double-diamond cage members with no hexagonal label.
=nih= counts hexagonal cage members with no double-diamond label.
=nmixed= counts members of both cage types.
=chillice= counts =CHILL+= cubic plus hexagonal molecules.
=chillmax= is the largest =CHILL+= bulk-ice cluster.
=chillinterfacial= counts =CHILL+= interfacial molecules.
=sixrings= counts primitive six-membered rings on the union graph.
=nionice= counts ions whose first water shell is all cage molecules.
=nionfront= counts ions with a mixed first shell.
=nionliq= counts ions with no cage molecule in the first shell.
=nclasses= counts distinct local topology classes on the mutual graph.
=nnamed= counts molecules whose local key the =LIBRARY= names.

Omit =IONS= and the three ion components stay 0. Omit =LIBRARY= and
=nnamed= stays 0.

* Ions

Ions stay off the hydrogen-bond network. =IONS= builds the graph on
the oxygen atoms and reads each ion against that assignment. Water
inside =ION_CUTOFF= of the ion forms the first shell. An ion sits in
ice if every shell molecule carries a cage label. It sits in liquid if
no shell molecule does. Mixed shells put the ion at the front. The
three ion components report brine rejection at a growing front in an
electrolyte, for example TIP4P/2005 with the Madrid 2019 ions.

#+begin_src plumed
LOAD FILE=libdseams_plumed.so
brine: DSEAMS_CAGES ATOMS=1-3000 IONS=3001-3060 ION_CUTOFF=3.5 COMPLETE
PRINT ARG=brine.nice,brine.nmax,brine.nionice,brine.nionfront,brine.nionliq STRIDE=500 FILE=BRINE
#+end_src

* Topology keys

Every molecule carries a local topology key: its rooted bonded
neighbourhood on the mutual graph within =HOPS= bonds (three by default).
=nclasses= counts the distinct keys in the frame. Point =LIBRARY= at a
key library written by =seams fingerprint --emit-library LABEL=, or by
the Python and Lua front ends. Then =nnamed= counts the molecules whose
key the library names. The same count names a hydrate framework or a
high-pressure ice.

#+begin_example
hyd: DSEAMS_CAGES ATOMS=1-2944 LIBRARY=sI_sII.keys,sI_sII_h2.keys HOPS=3
PRINT ARG=hyd.nnamed,hyd.nclasses STRIDE=500 FILE=HYDRATE
#+end_example

* Inside LAMMPS

Place =fix plumed= before =fix npt= or =fix press/berendsen=. Those
fixes compute the pressure internally. LAMMPS refuses a reversed
order. A minimal mW run logs the score every 500 steps. It stops on
a committor basin:

#+begin_src lammps
fix pl  all plumed plumedfile plumed.dat outfile plumed.log
fix npt all npt temp 215 215 1000 iso 1 1 10000
#+end_src

Use =plumed.dat= as above and =LENGTH_SCALE=1.0= after =UNITS LENGTH=A=.
LAMMPS =units real= matches the A unit of the engine.

* Driver tests

Meson registers the driver checks below. The ice, liquid and interface
frames are generated by =tests/chill_match.py= when the test runs. They
are not stored in the repository.

=driver_chill= compares =DSEAMS_CHILL= (published rule and
=ovito-chill+=) and =DSEAMS_RINGS= with =dseams_offline=, which calls
the same engine on the same xyz frames: ideal ice Ic, a hard-sphere
liquid, and an ice/liquid interface. It also checks that the smooth
q_3 matches =sph::spheriHarmo= and that the hard-shell correlations on
ice Ic match the published bonds.

=driver_smooth= checks that the smooth fraction is near one on ideal
ice Ic and lower on a disordered copy, that =plumed driver
--debug-forces= agrees with the analytic derivative, that =METAD=
accepts the smooth value, and that =METAD= refuses the discrete
fraction.

The older three checks are unchanged.

The nucleation check lives in =tests/driver_check.py=. It converts a
LAMMPS dump to xyz and runs the driver with the module loaded. Per
frame it compares =nice=, =nmax=, =chillice= and =chillmax= with the
engine =walk_compare= table. Absent a dump, the script uses the mW
cubic fixture under =DSEAMS_PLUMED_DATA= or the seams-core subproject.
The dump becomes xyz with the cell on the comment line. The check
does not depend on the molfile plugin.

#+begin_src bash
python tests/driver_check.py $(which plumed) build/libdseams_plumed.so \
    data/nucleation.lammpstrj results/walks/figshare-nucleation.txt
#+end_src

=driver_ions= (=tests/ion_check.py=) places two ions on a cubic-diamond
oxygen lattice. It checks =nionice= 2, =nionfront= 0, =nionliq= 0, with
=nice= still the remaining lattice.

=driver_library= (=tests/library_check.py=) writes a cubic-diamond
lattice, emits its key library with =seams fingerprint --emit-library=,
and checks that =nnamed= equals the molecule count and =nclasses= is
one. Meson registers this test when a =seams= binary is on =PATH=.

* Timing

One LAMMPS 2026.9.30 run of 4096 mW molecules on ideal ice Ic, units
real, timestep 5 fs, PLUMED 2.10.2 with one thread. The baseline
velocity-Verlet step, with no PLUMED, is 1.91 ms. The extra time per
step when the collective variable is evaluated every step, every 10
steps, and every 100 steps:

| collective variable | every step | every 10 | every 100 | one evaluation |
| DSEAMS_CHILL | 4.6 ms | 0.65 ms | 0.20 ms | 4.6 ms |
| DSEAMS_RINGS | 5.4 ms | 0.82 ms | 0.20 ms | 5.3 ms |
| DSEAMS_SMOOTH_CHILL, with the restraint | 2.6 ms | evaluated every step | evaluated every step | 2.6 ms |
| Q6 MEAN | 0.51 s | 51 ms | 5.1 ms | 0.51 s |
| LOCAL_Q6 MEAN | 6.4 s | 0.64 s | 64 ms | 6.5 s |
| ENVIRONMENTSIMILARITY DIAMOND | 0.22 s | 22 ms | 2.2 ms | 0.22 s |

Q6 and LOCAL_Q6 are the Steinhardt parameters that used to live in
the crystallization module. In PLUMED 2.10 they are the symfunc
module, and they need the adjmat contact matrix. ENVIRONMENTSIMILARITY
is the crystallization environment comparison, now the envsim module,
pointed at cubic diamond with the ice Ic lattice constant. On this
crystal the published fraction stays 1, the smooth value stays 0.989,
and the ring census is 8192 primitive six-rings. A restraint on the
smooth fraction forces an evaluation every step, so the 10- and
100-step intervals do not apply to it. Six repeats, PLUMED's own
forward-loop average, one thread and eight (=NUM_THREADS=). The two
counts agree bit for bit. Raw lines are under =bench/logs/threads/=.

| molecules | 1 thread | 8 threads |
| 4096 | 2.64 ms | 1.05 ms |
| 32768 | 24.4 ms | 9.21 ms |

The minimum step in those logs, after the neighbour list has been
built, is 19.5 ms and 5.1 ms at 32768 (3.8 times). The force loop
scales; the gather of Y_3m onto each molecule does not.

PRINT stride is the evaluation
interval when nothing else asks for the value: PLUMED skips the
calculation on the steps in between, and the extra time per step falls
by that factor.

A 512-molecule mW box with a crystalline core in liquid water is under
=examples/lammps-mw-freeze/=. At 230 K a moving restraint takes the
published ice fraction through 0.9 in 54 to 64 ps over three velocity
seeds. A 2 ns well-tempered metadynamics of the same collective
variable on the TIP4P/Ice slab is =examples/gromacs-tip4pice/plumed-metad.dat=.
